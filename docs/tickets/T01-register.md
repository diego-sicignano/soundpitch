# T01. Register and confirm eligibility

**Depends on:** none  **Acceptance:** none (manual)  **Rules:** 1, 3, 4, 4.12

## Goal

Be a registered entrant before **20 Oct 2026, 12:00 Pacific** (about 21:00 in Italy). Without it nothing can be submitted.

## Steps

1. Open <https://aka.ms/insidethegame> and click **Register**. If the page shows an error, try again later and from another browser; the rules name it as the place to register.
2. Decide: individual, or a team of up to four. A team needs one **Representative**.
3. Read section 3 (eligibility) and 4.12 (no financial or preferential support from Microsoft) and confirm they apply.
4. Note the date and time you registered in this file, under "Result". Do not put personal data or confirmation numbers in the repository.
