| AI-assisted development and ownership | §4, §4.6, §4.11 | **Rules:** nothing forbids AI coding assistants; §4 says developer tools are "subject to the license agreement related thereto"; GitHub Copilot is a judged technology (§4.2, §6). **Tool terms:** Anthropic's Consumer Terms (effective 8 Oct 2025) assign to the user "all our right, title, and interest (if any) in Outputs"; the Commercial Terms say the customer "owns its Outputs" (both read). **Italian law** (the project is made in Italy): legge 132/2025, art. 25, in force since 10 Oct 2025, amends art. 1 of legge 633/1941 so that works are protected "anche laddove create con l'ausilio di strumenti di intelligenza artificiale, purché costituenti risultato del lavoro intellettuale dell'autore" (Gazzetta Ufficiale text read). **US view:** the Copyright Office's press release of 29 Jan 2025 says outputs are protectable "only where a human author has determined sufficient expressive elements", not by "the mere provision of prompts", and that AI assistance "does not bar copyrightability" (press release and the Library of Congress summary read). Re-checked on 2026-10-21 through search extracts of Part 2 of the Office's report (published at copyright.gov/ai/): prompts alone do not give enough control for authorship, choosing among outputs is not authorship, and human-authored material, or a creative arrangement or modification of an output, is protectable; the Register's May 2026 Senate testimony repeats it. Extracts only: the full report was not read. ## What this record does not settle

- Whether the organisers read §4.11 ("solely owned") to exclude work written with an AI assistant. Nothing in the rules says so and the rules themselves promote AI tooling, so the project discloses it plainly and does not try to hide it.
- How the Sponsor reads the use of about USD 0.05 of Azure for Students credit against rule 4.12 (see the billing row and D23): the evidence is complete, the reading is the Sponsor's.
- Anything about the owner's personal circumstances (age, residence, employment, registration), which only the owner can attest.
