- How the Sponsor reads the use of about USD 0.05 of Azure for Students credit against rule 4.12 (see the billing row and D23): the evidence is complete, the reading is the Sponsor's.
- Anything about the owner's personal circumstances (age, residence, employment, registration), which only the owner can attest.
